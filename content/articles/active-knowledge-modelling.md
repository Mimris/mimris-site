# What Is Active Knowledge Modelling?

> Draft canonical article for `/active-knowledge-modelling` and future editorial use.

Active Knowledge Modelling (AKM) is the practice of creating connected, evolvable models that remain useful as context for people, software, and AI.

Traditional models can become passive records: carefully produced, published, and then separated from daily work. An active model can be explored, challenged, connected to evidence, used in decisions, and revised as understanding develops.

The progression is:

```text
Information -> Knowledge -> Models -> Context -> Human + AI work -> Results
```

Mimris uses this approach across a shared Universe. Mimris Modelling creates and evolves the structure. Mimris AI Workspace uses that structure with documents, processes, tasks, decisions, and deliverables.

The Coffee Shop Universe makes the idea concrete: a familiar domain connects people, products, systems, processes, information, views, and work instead of leaving them as disconnected fragments.
