# Mimris Modelling and Mimris AI Workspace: Two Complementary Jobs

> Draft canonical article for `/products` and future editorial use.

Mimris Modelling and Mimris AI Workspace belong to one ecosystem, but they solve different problems.

**Mimris Modelling** creates, structures, visualizes, inspects, and evolves model semantics, objects, relationships, diagrams, and views. Its question is: *What is the world, and how is it structured?*

**Mimris AI Workspace** uses structured models with documents, processes, tasks, decisions, and deliverables. Its question is: *What do we do within the world we have modelled?*

The boundary is practical: capabilities that primarily manipulate model structure belong in Mimris Modelling; capabilities that primarily help people or AI perform work using models and context belong in Mimris AI Workspace.

Together they support the short Mimris message: **Model the world. Work with it.**
