# Mimris Modelling and Mimris AI Workspace: Two Complementary Jobs

> Draft canonical article for `/products` and future editorial use.

Mimris Modelling and Mimris AI Workspace belong to one ecosystem, but they solve different problems.

**Mimris Modelling** is the specialist environment for visualizing, managing, inspecting, and evolving model semantics, objects, relationships, diagrams, and views. Its question is: *What is the world, and how is it structured?*

**Mimris AI Workspace** runs the Active Knowledge Modelling method in a working context. It connects models with documents, evidence, processes, tasks, decisions, deliverables, and AI-assisted collaboration. Its question is: *What do we do within the world we have modelled, and what do we learn as we work?*

The boundary is practical: Mimris Modelling provides the dedicated tools to visualize and manage model structure; Mimris AI Workspace runs AKM through ongoing work, using models as context and allowing work to reveal proposed changes and new knowledge.

Together they support the short Mimris message: **Model the world. Work with it.**
